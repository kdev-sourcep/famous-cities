      Seoul

      South Korea's high-tech capital mixes 600-year-old palaces and hanok villages with K-pop, night markets and some of the fastest internet on Earth. It is ranked #9 on Time Out's Best Cities 2026 list.

      Overview

      Why visit Seoul

      Seoul has been Korea's capital for more than 600 years, since the Joseon dynasty moved its court here in 1394. Five grand palaces remain in the city centre, including Gyeongbokgung, where the changing of the guard reenactment draws crowds daily.

      Modern Seoul never sits still. Neon-lit districts like Myeongdong and Gangnam hum with shops and K-pop, the futuristic Dongdaemun Design Plaza anchors the design district, and views from N Seoul Tower on Namsan Mountain stretch for miles.

      Eating is a highlight: grill bulgogi at a Korean barbecue table, snack on tteokbokki at Gwangjang Market, and try fried chicken with beer — chimaek — like a local. A day trip to the DMZ border or a hike up Bukhansan's granite peaks adds history and nature to the mix.

      Fun facts

      Things you didn't know about Seoul

      1. 01

        Capital For Over 600 Years

        Seoul became Korea's capital in 1394 under the Joseon dynasty and still holds five royal palaces.

      2. 02

        A River Runs Through It

        The Han River splits Seoul in two and is lined with parks that fill with picnickers and cyclists.

      3. 03

        A Highway Became A Stream

        The elevated Cheonggye freeway was removed in 2005 to restore the Cheonggyecheon stream through downtown.

      4. 04

        The Palace Of Shining Happiness

        Gyeongbokgung, built in 1395, is the largest of Seoul's royal palaces.

      5. 05

        World-Class Internet

        Seoul consistently ranks among the cities with the world's fastest internet speeds.

      6. 06

        A Mountain In The City

        Bukhansan National Park sits inside the city limits and is among the world's most-visited national parks per unit area.

      7. 07

        Shopping That Never Sleeps

        Dongdaemun's fashion district and malls famously stay open through the night.

      8. 08

        A Rainbow Fountain Bridge

        Banpo Bridge's Moonlight Rainbow Fountain shoots water from both sides of the bridge in rainbow colors.

      9. 09

        Real-Life Gangnam

        Gangnam, made world-famous by Psy's 2012 hit, is a real district — and a hub for K-pop entertainment companies.

      10. 10

        A Border Day Trip

        The DMZ separating North and South Korea lies about an hour north, and tours visit observation posts and tunnels.
