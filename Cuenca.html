CUENCA











POPULATION

METRO: 600,000

Ecuador's most beautiful colonial city drifts along the Tomebamba river beneath blue cathedral domes — and it's where the Panama hat was actually born.

HIGHLIGHT RANKING

Methodology

Cuenca sits at 2,560 metres in the southern Andes, a city of cobbled lanes, flower markets and carved balconies that earned UNESCO World Heritage status for its intact colonial centre. The twin blue domes of the New Cathedral dominate the skyline.

Four rivers thread the city, and the Tomebamba's grassy banks are where Cuencanos stroll, picnic and cycle. The markets brim with roses and orchids, the craft stalls sell toquilla 'Panama' hats — an Ecuadorian invention named for the canal workers who bought them — and pumapungo ruins sit inside the city.

The surrounding mountains deliver the day trips: Cajas National Park's 200+ glacial lakes an hour away, the Inca ruins of Ingapirca — Ecuador's most important — to the north, and the artisan towns of Gualaceo and Chordeleg famous for silver filigree and weavings.

FUN FACTS

PANAMA HATS ARE ECUADORIAN

The toquilla straw hat was made in Ecuador for centuries — canal workers made 'Panama' the name stick.

A CITY OF RIVERS

Four rivers — Tomebamba, Yanuncay, Tarqui and Machángara — all meet inside the city.

BLUE DOMES

The New Cathedral's blue-and-white domes, covered in Czech tile, took a century to finish.

AN INCA SITE IN TOWN

The Pumapungo ruins in the city centre were once Huayna Capac's palace complex.

200 GLACIAL LAKES

Cajas National Park's high plateau is scattered with over 200 lakes an hour from town.

ECUADOR'S INGAP RCA

Ingapirca, the country's biggest Inca ruins, has a perfectly elliptical Temple of the Sun.

THE RETIREES' FAVOURITE

Cuenca repeatedly tops lists of the world's best places to retire for its climate and cost.

A CITY OF ARTISANS

Neighbouring Chordeleg and Gualaceo are centres of silver filigree, ceramics and ikat weaving.

THE FLOWER MARKET

Plaza de las Flores beside the cathedral sells roses and orchids grown in the valley.

A NAME TO REMEMBER

The full name is Santa Ana de los Cuatro Ríos de Cuenca — Saint Anne of the Four Rivers.
