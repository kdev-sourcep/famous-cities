TALLINN











POPULATION

METRO: 450,000

The Baltic's medieval jewel — a walled old town of towers and cobbles that runs one of the world's most digital countries.

HIGHLIGHT RANKING

Methodology

Tallinn's Old Town is one of Europe's best-preserved medieval centres — a UNESCO-listed maze of guild houses, merchant towers and intact city walls that survived the 20th century almost untouched.

Up on Toompea hill, the Estonian parliament sits in a pink baroque castle above Orthodox Alexander Nevsky Cathedral's onion domes; below, the lower town's Raekoja plats has hosted markets for 800 years. Beyond the walls, Telliskivi's former factories are now the creative city — street art, food halls and startups.

Speed's 2024 tour streamed from Tallinn. It's also Europe's digital pioneer — e-residency, paperless government and the birthplace of Skype — making the medieval streets an oddly fitting HQ for a tech nation.

FUN FACTS

BEST-PRESERVED MEDIEVAL TOWN

The old town's walls, towers and lanes survived two world wars mostly intact.

2 KM OF WALL

About half the medieval city wall still stands — with 20 towers you can visit.

SKYPE'S BIRTHPLACE

Skype was built in Tallinn in 2003 — seeding Estonia's startup scene.

E-RESIDENCY

Estonia offers digital residency to anyone — the world's first country to do so.

A PINK PARLIAMENT

Toompea Castle, housing parliament, is painted baroque pink.

800-YEAR-OLD SQUARE

Raekoja plats has run a market since the 13th century — Christmas market included.

THE OLDEST PHARMACY

Town Hall Pharmacy has operated continuously since 1422.

TWO TOWNS IN ONE

Upper (nobles') Toompea and lower (merchants') town were once rival cities.

SINGING REVOLUTION

Estonia's path to freedom was literally sung — mass choirs defied the Soviets.

TELLISKIVI

The creative city in old railway factories is the hip district's engine.
