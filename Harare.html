HARARE











POPULATION

METRO: 3,000,000

The sunshine city of jacarandas — gateway to Victoria Falls, Great Zimbabwe's medieval ruins and the Eastern Highlands' tea country.

HIGHLIGHT RANKING

Methodology

Harare is the 'sunshine city' — an upland capital of jacaranda-lined avenues that turn purple each October, built on a plateau that keeps the climate mild year-round.

The city's sights centre on the National Gallery's sculpture collection (Zimbabwe's stone sculptors are world-famous), the Mbare market's energy, and the granite kopjes rising through the suburbs. The balancing rocks — the symbol on the old banknotes — stand at the city's edge.

Speed's 2025 Africa tour streamed from Harare. Zimbabwe's real icons surround it: Victoria Falls up the road, Great Zimbabwe's 11th-century stone city (the country's namesake) to the south, and the Eastern Highlands' misty tea estates to the east.

FUN FACTS

JACARANDA CITY

October turns the avenues purple — Harare's famous seasonal show.

GREAT ZIMBABWE

The medieval stone city gave the country its name — 'houses of stone'.

THE SUNSHINE CITY

The 1,480m altitude keeps the climate mild and bright.

BALANCING ROCKS

The granite formations so famous they appeared on the currency.

STONE SCULPTURE

Zimbabwe's Shona sculptors are collected worldwide — the National Gallery shows why.

MBARE MARKET

The country's oldest township market is a sensory overload.

THE SMOKING THUNDER

Victoria Falls — one of the world's largest — is a flight or drive northwest.

EASTERN HIGHLANDS

Tea estates, misty forests and trout streams cool the eastern border.

A LION & ELEPHANT COUNTRY

Hwange's massive elephant herds are a top safari extension.

KOPJES IN THE CITY

Granite whaleback hills rise right through the suburbs.
