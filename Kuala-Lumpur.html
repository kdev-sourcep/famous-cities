      Kuala Lumpur

      Petronas Twin Towers over a street-food city — Batu Caves' rainbow stairs, the night markets of Jalan Alor and three cultures layered into one skyline.

      Overview

      Why visit Kuala Lumpur

      Kuala Lumpur mixes Malay, Chinese and Indian worlds in a single skyline crowned by the Petronas Twin Towers — the tallest twin buildings on earth, joined by a skybridge on the 41st floor.

      The city's soul is at street level: Jalan Alor's nightly food market, the red lanterns of Petaling Street, and the colonial-era Sultan Abdul Samad buildings at Merdeka Square, where independence was declared in 1957.

      Fifteen minutes north, Batu Caves' 272 rainbow steps climb to Hindu temples inside a limestone cave, guarded by a 43-metre golden Murugan and a troupe of monkeys. Speed's 2024 tour stop drew some of the biggest crowds of the whole trip.

      Fun facts

      Things you didn't know about Kuala Lumpur

      1. 01

        The Tallest Twins

        The Petronas Towers were the world's tallest buildings 1998–2004 — still the tallest twins.

      2. 02

        Rainbow Stairs

        Batu Caves' 272 rainbow-painted steps became an instant icon in 2018.

      3. 03

        Three Cultures, One City

        Mosques, Hindu temples and Chinese clan houses sit streets apart.

      4. 04

        A Nightly Food Court

        Jalan Alor packs about 200 stalls of satay, noodles and durian every night.

      5. 05

        Merdeka Square

        Independence was proclaimed here in 1957 — the flagpole is among the world's tallest.

      6. 06

        A Muddy Name

        'Kuala Lumpur' means 'muddy confluence' — two muddy rivers built a metropolis.

      7. 07

        Fireflies Nearby

        Kuala Selangor's firefly colonies glow an hour from the towers.

      8. 08

        Rainforest Inside

        KL Forest Eco Park keeps primary rainforest in the city centre.

      9. 09

        Monkey Ushers

        Batu Caves' macaques escort — and rob — pilgrims climbing the stairs.

      10. 10

        The Crossroads

        KL sits midway between Singapore and Bangkok, Southeast Asia's natural hub.

      FAQ

      Quick answers about Kuala Lumpur

      Where is Kuala Lumpur located?

      Kuala Lumpur is in Malaysia, in Asia.

      What is the population of Kuala Lumpur?

      The greater Kuala Lumpur area is home to about 8,800,000 people.

      What is Kuala Lumpur famous for?

      Kuala Lumpur is famous for the Petronas Twin Towers, Batu Caves' rainbow steps and the Jalan Alor night food market.
