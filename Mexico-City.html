      Mexico City

      One of the world's largest cities layers Aztec ruins, grand colonial squares, floating gardens and what many call the planet's best street food. It is ranked #7 on Time Out's Best Cities 2026 list.

      Overview

      Why visit Mexico City

      Mexico City was founded as Tenochtitlan in 1325, the island capital of the Aztec Empire. After the Spanish conquest in 1521 the city was rebuilt on the ruins — the Metropolitan Cathedral stands beside the excavated Templo Mayor on the vast Zócalo square.

      The city is packed with world-class museums. The National Museum of Anthropology tells Mexico's story, the blue Casa Azul was Frida Kahlo's home, and the Palacio de Bellas Artes holds murals by Rivera and Orozco. The leafy neighborhoods of Roma and Condesa are full of cafes, galleries and restaurants.

      Food here is legendary, from tacos al pastor on street corners to high-end tasting menus. Ride a colorful trajinera boat through the canals of Xochimilco, day-trip to the pyramids of Teotihuacan, and catch lucha libre wrestling at Arena México.

      Fun facts

      Things you didn't know about Mexico City

      1. 01

        Built On A Lake

        The Aztec capital Tenochtitlan stood on an island in Lake Texcoco. The modern city is slowly sinking on the soft lakebed soil.

      2. 02

        Pyramids Next Door

        Teotihuacan, with its Pyramid of the Sun and Pyramid of the Moon, lies about an hour outside the city and predates the Aztecs.

      3. 03

        Museums Galore

        Mexico City has well over 150 museums, among the most of any city in the world.

      4. 04

        North America'S Oldest University

        The Royal and Pontifical University of Mexico was founded in 1551, more than 80 years before Harvard.

      5. 05

        Floating Gardens

        The canals and chinampas of Xochimilco are a UNESCO World Heritage Site, still used to grow flowers and vegetables.

      6. 06

        One Of The World'S Biggest Cities

        Greater Mexico City is home to more than 20 million people, making it one of the largest urban areas on Earth.

      7. 07

        The Palace Of Fine Arts

        The Palacio de Bellas Artes has a marble exterior and a famous stained-glass stage curtain.

      8. 08

        A Real Royal Castle

        Chapultepec Castle is the only castle in the Americas to have housed reigning monarchs.

      9. 09

        A Giant Main Square

        The Zócalo is one of the largest city squares in the world.

      10. 10

        High Altitude

        At about 2,240 metres above sea level, Mexico City sits higher than most capitals — some visitors feel the altitude.
