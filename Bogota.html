BOGOTÁ











POPULATION

METRO: 11,000,000

Colombia's high-altitude capital mixes a gold museum, graffiti-covered colonial lanes and a mountain-top church you reach by cable car. It is ranked #50 on Time Out's Best Cities 2026 list.

HIGHLIGHT RANKING

Methodology

Bogotá sits on a high Andean plateau at 2,600 metres. Founded in 1538, it became the capital of Gran Colombia and today sprawls between mountains in a grid of neighbourhoods, each with its own feel — from cobbled La Candelaria to chic Zona T.

The Museo del Oro holds the world's largest collection of pre-Hispanic gold, including the raft that gave rise to the El Dorado legend. Above the city, the funicular and cable car climb to Monserrate's white church for a view over the whole plateau.

Bogotá is South America's street-art capital — the legal walls of La Candelaria and beyond are an open-air gallery. Sundays transform the city when the Ciclovía closes 120 kilometres of roads to cars, and nightlife peaks at Theatron, one of Latin America's biggest clubs. A day trip to Zipaquirá's salt cathedral — a church carved inside a salt mine — is unmissable.

FUN FACTS

THE EL DORADO MUSEUM

The Museo del Oro holds over 30,000 gold objects, including the Muisca raft that inspired the El Dorado legend.

A CITY IN THE CLOUDS

At 2,640 metres, Bogotá is the third-highest capital city in South America.

A CHURCH INSIDE A MINE

Zipaquirá's Salt Cathedral is carved 180 metres underground in a working salt mine.

SUNDAYS WITHOUT CARS

The Ciclovía has closed city streets to traffic every Sunday since 1974 — a model copied worldwide.

A STREET-ART CAPITAL

Graffiti was decriminalised in Bogotá, turning whole districts into galleries of world-class murals.

A CABLE-CAR CHURCH

The white church on Monserrate has watched over the city since the 1600s.

THE LATIN AMERICAN ATHENS

Bogotá earned the nickname for its universities, libraries and cultural life.

HOT CHOCOLATE WITH CHEESE

The city's signature snack is hot chocolate served with a cube of cheese to melt inside.

AN AIRPORT ABOVE THE TREES

El Dorado airport, named for the legend, is one of Latin America's busiest.

A MOUNTAIN IN MINUTES

Monserrate's summit sits 600 metres above the streets — walkable, funicular or cable car.
