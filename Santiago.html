SANTIAGO











POPULATION

METRO: 7,000,000

The Andes rise 3,000 metres straight out of the suburbs — a modern capital ringed by vineyards and ski slopes.

HIGHLIGHT RANKING

Methodology

Santiago sits in a bowl of mountains — the snow-capped Andes wall the east so steeply that ski resorts operate 40 minutes from downtown. The city is Chile's powerhouse: a third of the country lives here.

The centre balances grit and grace: the colonial Plaza de Armas and cathedral, La Moneda palace (bombed in the 1973 coup, now peaceful), and the bohemian Lastarria quarter of cafés and street art. Cerro San Cristóbal's hilltop Virgin overlooks it all — ride the funicular or cable car for the panorama.

Speed's 2025 South America tour streamed from Santiago to huge Chilean crowds. The city's other superpower is location — the Maipo and Colchagua wine valleys, Valparaíso's port and the Andes are all day trips.

FUN FACTS

ANDES ON THE SKYLINE

3,000m peaks rise over the eastern suburbs — a backdrop no other big city matches.

A THIRD OF CHILE

Roughly 7 million of Chile's 19 million live in Greater Santiago.

SKI IN THE MORNING

Valle Nevado and Portillo resorts are under two hours from downtown.

SAN CRISTÓBAL

The 22m Virgin statue crowns a hill twice Eiffel's height — funicular up.

LA MONEDA

The presidential palace survived the 1973 coup's bombing — its changing of the guard remains.

WINE COUNTRY

Maipo, Colchagua and Casablanca valleys put world-class reds within an hour.

THE POET'S CITY

Neruda's Santiago house, La Chascona, is a ship-shaped museum in Bellavista.

TALLEST IN SOUTH AMERICA

The Gran Torre Santiago — 300m — is the continent's tallest building.

THE SMOG TRAP

The mountain bowl traps winter smog — crisp blue-sky days follow wind or rain.

EMPANADA CAPITAL

Pino-filled baked empanadas are the city's hand-meal.
