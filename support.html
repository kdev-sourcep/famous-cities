Support Famous Cities and Its Creator

I’m raising support for myself as the independent creator behind Famous Cities, an independent website I built to explore notable cities through their landmarks, histories, cultures, architecture, and unique identities. I created this project because of my personal interest in cities, and I want to keep expanding it into a larger and more engaging experience. Working on Famous Cities has given me a stronger sense of purpose, lets me share one of my hobbies with others, and has become a passive way for me to make income through hotel widget commissions on the site.

This fundraiser is personal support for me as I continue building and maintaining Famous Cities. Donations can give me greater freedom to keep researching, writing, programming, maintaining affiliate programs, improving the design, and developing new website content. Most donations may be used at my discretion for general personal expenses, which helps me continue putting time and energy into this project. A smaller portion may go directly toward website-related costs such as hosting, programming resources, affiliate-program expenses, research, digital assets, IP address expenses, and affiliate program costs for TravelPayouts and Stay22.

Because this is an ongoing creative project, the need for support is ongoing as well. Predictable recurring donations would help me continue covering the personal and website expenses that come with keeping Famous Cities active and growing over time. At the same time, one-time donations are also sincerely appreciated and make a real difference.

This is a personal fundraiser supporting me as an independent creator. Donations are voluntary gifts and do not provide products, services, website ownership, investment rights, profit participation, or financial returns.

If you enjoy Famous Cities, appreciate independent creative projects, or simply want to support me and my work, please consider contributing. Every donation is appreciated, regardless of size, and sharing this fundraiser with people interested in cities, culture, history, architecture, or travel would also help tremendously. If the website grows enough and I have the means in the future, I hope to be in a position to give back generously to others as well. Thank you for supporting me and the continued growth of Famous Cities.
