IShowSpeed’s World Tour

Darren “IShowSpeed” Watkins Jr. is one of the biggest livestreamers on the planet — and since 2024 he’s been turning entire countries into his stage, streaming IRL adventures to millions of viewers in real time. Below is every leg of his travels, with our full travel guides linked for the cities he streamed from.

Europe Tour (2024)

Speed’s first massive international run — a sweep across Western Europe, the Nordics and the Balkans that included the famous Oslo crowd incident and dozens of chaotic street streams.

London · Paris · Amsterdam · Munich · Oslo · Stockholm · Copenhagen · Helsinki · Tallinn · Riga · Vilnius · Warsaw · Budapest · Bucharest · Belgrade · Sofia · Sarajevo · Skopje · Tirana

Southeast Asia Tour (2024)

The elephant ride, the tuk-tuk crash, the Manny Pacquiao boxing session — this leg produced some of his most-clipped moments ever.

Bangkok · Singapore · Manila · Kuala Lumpur · Jakarta · Hanoi · Phnom Penh

Australia & New Zealand (2024)

Speed headed down under in late 2024, packing out malls and landmarks across Australia and New Zealand.

Sydney · Melbourne · Brisbane · Perth · Adelaide · Auckland · Queenstown

South America Tour (2025)

Massive crowds met Speed across South America — football culture in Buenos Aires and Rio, and a trek up to Machu Picchu.

Buenos Aires · Rio de Janeiro · Lima · Cusco · Bogotá · Santiago

China & Mongolia Tour (2025)

The Great Wall backflip, kung-fu training at the Shaolin Temple, and the Chongqing drone show that broke the internet.

Beijing · Shanghai · Hong Kong · Shenzhen · Chongqing · Changsha · Zhengzhou · Ulaanbaatar

Africa Tour (2025)

Speed’s longest tour yet — around twenty countries in about a month, from souks to safaris.

Cape Town · Cairo · Marrakesh · Nairobi · Lagos · Accra · Dakar · Addis Ababa · Luanda · Maputo · Windhoek · Gaborone · Lusaka · Harare

Japan & Korea (2023–2024)

Before the mega-tours, Speed’s Asia trips — sumo stables in Tokyo and chaos in Seoul — proved the format worked.

Tokyo · Seoul · Osaka
