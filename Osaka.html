      Osaka

      Japan's kitchen — where Dotonbori's giant neon crab guards a canyon of street food and the castle looms over the nation's funniest city.

      Overview

      Why visit Osaka

      Osaka runs on eating. The city's motto is kuidaore — 'eat until you drop' — and Dotonbori delivers: a canal-side canyon of giant neon signs (the running Glico man, a mechanical crab) above takoyaki stands and ramen counters that stay loud past midnight.

      Osaka Castle's reconstructed keep rises over moats and plum groves where Tokugawa history played out; Shinsekai's retro district surrounds the Tsutenkaku tower like a living Showa-era postcard; and Universal Studios Japan draws the theme-park crowds.

      Speed streamed from Osaka on his Japan leg, mobbed through the Dotonbori crowds. Kyoto's temples are 15 minutes by bullet train — but Osaka's grub-first culture is the experience.

      Fun facts

      Things you didn't know about Osaka

      1. 01

        Kuidaore City

        Osaka's motto is 'eat until you drop' — the food obsession is the identity.

      2. 02

        The Giant Crab

        Dotonbori's mechanical king crab sign has waved its claws since the 1960s.

      3. 03

        Glico Man

        The running-man billboard has lit the canal since 1935 — Osaka's unofficial mascot.

      4. 04

        Takoyaki Home

        Octopus balls were invented here — vendors flip them with tattoo speed.

      5. 05

        Osaka Castle

        Toyotomi Hideyoshi's 16th-century fortress — rebuilt, it anchors the skyline.

      6. 06

        Japan'S Kitchen

        The nickname dates to the Edo period, when Osaka was the nation's rice-trading hub.

      7. 07

        Comedy Capital

        Manzai stand-up comedy was born here — Osaka produces Japan's biggest comedians.

      8. 08

        Shinsekai

        The 'New World' district preserves 1912 nostalgia under the Tsutenkaku tower.

      9. 09

        Usj

        Universal Studios Japan holds the Nintendo World land that opened to fan frenzy.

      10. 10

        Kushikatsu Rule

        Fried skewer restaurants warn: no double-dipping in the shared sauce.

      FAQ

      Quick answers about Osaka

      Where is Osaka located?

      Osaka is in Japan, in Asia.

      What is the population of Osaka?

      The greater Osaka area is home to about 19,000,000 people.

      What is Osaka famous for?

      Osaka is famous for Japan's kitchen — Dotonbori's neon food strip, takoyaki, Osaka Castle and the country's loudest comedy culture.

      Did IShowSpeed stream from Osaka?

      Yes — IShowSpeed streamed from Osaka on his Japan tour, mobbed through the Dotonbori crowds. See his full world tour map.
