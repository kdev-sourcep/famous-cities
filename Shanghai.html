      Shanghai

      China's biggest city stacks a futuristic Pudong skyline over the historic Bund, with tree-lined French Concession streets, famous soup dumplings and the fastest commercial train on Earth. It is ranked #2 on Time Out's Best Cities 2026 list.

      Overview

      Why visit Shanghai

      Shanghai grew from a fishing town into a giant treaty port after 1842, when foreign concessions gave it grand European-style buildings along the Bund. By the 1930s it was known as the 'Paris of the East', famous for glamour, jazz and Art Deco towers.

      Across the Huangpu River, the Pudong district shows the modern city: the Oriental Pearl Tower, the bottle-opener-shaped World Financial Center and the twisting Shanghai Tower, one of the tallest buildings on Earth. Back on the west bank, the Ming-era Yu Garden and the former French Concession's leafy avenues keep the old city alive.

      Shanghai is a food city at heart. Order xiaolongbao, the delicate soup dumplings born in nearby Nanxiang, snack through the Old Town bazaar, and take a Huangpu River night cruise to see both skylines light up. Day trips reach the canals of Zhujiajiao water town or Shanghai Disneyland.

      Fun facts

      Things you didn't know about Shanghai

      1. 01

        A Towering Skyline

        The 632-metre Shanghai Tower is one of the tallest buildings in the world, and its elevators are among the fastest ever built.

      2. 02

        The Bund'S Waterfront

        The Bund's riverside promenade is lined with more than 50 grand colonial-era buildings in Gothic, Baroque and Art Deco styles.

      3. 03

        Paris Of The East

        In the 1920s and 1930s Shanghai was a global centre of fashion, jazz and nightlife, earning its famous nickname.

      4. 04

        Soup Dumplings

        Xiaolongbao, steamed dumplings filled with pork and hot broth, were invented in Nanxiang, now part of Shanghai.

      5. 05

        The Fastest Train

        The Shanghai Maglev reaches 431 km/h and covers the 30 kilometres from the airport to the city in about 8 minutes.

      6. 06

        A Metro Giant

        The Shanghai Metro runs more than 800 kilometres of track, making it one of the longest metro systems in the world.

      7. 07

        A 400-Year-Old Garden

        Yu Garden was laid out during the Ming dynasty in 1559 and still sits beside the bustling Old Town bazaar.

      8. 08

        Disney'S Biggest Castle

        Shanghai Disneyland's Enchanted Storybook Castle is the largest Disney castle ever built.

      9. 09

        The World'S Busiest Port

        The Port of Shanghai has handled more shipping containers than any other port every year since 2010.

      10. 10

        A Name That Means 'Above The Sea'

        Shanghai sits on the Yangtze River Delta where the river meets the East China Sea.
