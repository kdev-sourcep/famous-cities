      Best Cities to Visit in India

      India's cities are some of the most exciting on Earth: Mughal monuments, holy rivers, royal palaces and incredible street food. Here are ten to plan a trip around.

      1. 01

        New Delhi India

        The capital is the start of most trips, with the Red Fort, Qutub Minar, India Gate and the food stalls of Old Delhi.

        New Delhi guideHotelsTours
      2. 02

        Agra India

        Home of the Taj Mahal, plus Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri nearby. Go at sunrise for the best light and the smallest crowds.

        Agra guideHotelsTours
      3. 03

        Jaipur India

        The Pink City has Amer Fort, the Hawa Mahal and colourful bazaars. With Delhi and Agra it forms the classic Golden Triangle.

        Jaipur guideHotelsTours
      4. 04

        Varanasi India

        One of the world's oldest living cities. Watch the evening Ganga Aarti and take a sunrise boat ride along the ghats.

        Varanasi guideHotelsTours
      5. 05

        Mumbai India

        India's biggest city has the Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Bollywood and some of the country's best street food.

        Mumbai guideHotelsTours
      6. 06

        Kolkata India

        The cultural capital, with the Victoria Memorial, colonial streets, book markets and famous sweets.

        Kolkata guideHotelsTours
      7. 07

        Hyderabad India

        The City of Pearls is known for the Charminar, Golconda Fort and its famous Hyderabadi biryani.

        Hyderabad guideHotelsTours
      8. 08

        Chennai India

        The gateway to South India, with Marina Beach, great temples and classical music and dance.

        Chennai guideHotelsTours
      9. 09

        Bengaluru India

        A green, easygoing tech city with leafy parks, a pleasant climate and a famous craft beer and café scene.

        Bengaluru guideHotelsTours
      10. 10

        Ahmedabad India

        India's first UNESCO World Heritage City, with a historic walled old town, the Sabarmati Ashram and a riverfront.

        Ahmedabad guideHotelsTours

      FAQ

      Quick answers

      What is the Golden Triangle?

      The classic first-time route linking New Delhi, Agra and Jaipur. It takes about a week, and each leg is a few hours by road or train.

      When is the best time to visit Indian cities?

      October to March is cooler and drier for most of northern India. The summer months are very hot and the monsoon arrives around June.
