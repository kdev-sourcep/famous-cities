      Famous Football Cities and Stadiums

      For football fans, a match or stadium tour can be the highlight of a trip. These cities have the most famous clubs, stadiums and atmospheres in the world.

      1. 01

        Madrid Spain

        Tour the Santiago Bernabéu, home of Real Madrid, or catch Atlético at their Metropolitano stadium.

        Madrid guideHotelsTours
      2. 02

        Barcelona Spain

        FC Barcelona's Camp Nou is one of the largest stadiums in Europe, and the club museum is one of the city's most visited.

        Barcelona guideHotelsTours
      3. 03

        London United Kingdom

        More professional clubs than any other city, plus Wembley Stadium, the home of English football.

        London guideHotelsTours
      4. 04

        Munich Germany

        Bayern Munich's Allianz Arena glows red at night and has a big club museum.

        Munich guideHotelsTours
      5. 05

        Buenos Aires Argentina

        Feel the passion at Boca Juniors' La Bombonera or River Plate's Monumental, home of one of football's biggest rivalries.

        Buenos Aires guideHotelsTours
      6. 06

        Rio de Janeiro Brazil

        The Maracanã hosted World Cup finals in 1950 and 2014 and is a must for any fan.

        Rio de Janeiro guideHotelsTours
      7. 07

        Istanbul Türkiye

        Galatasaray, Fenerbahçe and Beşiktaş create some of the loudest derbies in the world.

        Istanbul guideHotelsTours
      8. 08

        Lisbon Portugal

        Benfica and Sporting share one of Europe's oldest rivalries, and both offer stadium tours.

        Lisbon guideHotelsTours
      9. 09

        Mexico City Mexico

        Estadio Azteca hosted the World Cup finals in 1970 and 1986 and is the venue for the opening match of the 2026 World Cup.

        Mexico City guideHotelsTours

      FAQ

      Quick answers

      How do I get match tickets?

      Buy from the club's official website or ticket office. Many stadiums also run tours on non-match days, which are easier to book.
