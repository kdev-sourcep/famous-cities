SHENZHEN











POPULATION

METRO: 17,500,000

China's 'instant city' went from fishing villages to a 17-million tech metropolis in forty years — gadget megamarkets, drone shows and theme parks beside Hong Kong.

HIGHLIGHT RANKING

Methodology

Shenzhen is the city China built in a hurry. A sleepy border county of fishing villages until 1980, it became the country's first Special Economic Zone and exploded into a metropolis of some 17 million — home to the companies behind the phones, drones and gadgets in your pocket, from Huawei and Tencent to DJI.

The city rewards the curious: Huaqiangbei is the world's largest electronics market, a multi-block maze where anything with a circuit can be bought, built or fixed. Around it are design museums, the Ping An tower's observation deck and a string of beaches on the Dapeng Peninsula.

Speed visited on his 2025 China tour, drawn by the gadget markets and the city's drone-light spectacles. Between OCT Loft's art district and malls the size of towns, this is the future arriving early.

FUN FACTS

AN INSTANT MEGACITY

Shenzhen grew from ~30,000 people in 1980 to over 17 million — the fastest city growth ever recorded.

SILICON VALLEY OF HARDWARE

Huaqiangbei market is the world's biggest electronics bazaar — prototypes get built there in days.

DRONE CAPITAL

DJI, the world's dominant drone maker, is headquartered in Shenzhen.

130 LANDMARKS IN ONE PARK

Window of the World squeezes replicas of 130 global icons into a single theme park.

HONG KONG'S NEIGHBOUR

The Hong Kong border is minutes away by metro or high-speed rail.

A CITY OF TWENTYSOMETHINGS

One of China's youngest populations built it — almost everyone migrated here.

SKY-BRIDGE TOWERS

Tencent's twin headquarters are linked by sky bridges holding gyms and running tracks.

OCT LOFT

A former factory zone turned arts district of murals, cafés and design shops.

CITY BEACHES

Dameisha and Xiaomeisha beaches sit inside the city on the South China Sea.

AN URBAN RAINFOREST

Mangrove reserves inside the megacity protect migratory birds on the flyway.
